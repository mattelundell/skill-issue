---step---
type: text

**Power** is how fast energy is used, measured in watts (W) or kilowatts (kW).
**Energy** is how much is used in total, measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

---step---
type: example

A 2 kW fan heater running for 3 hours uses 2 kW × 3 h = 6 kWh.
